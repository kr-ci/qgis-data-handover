about=Someone needs the data from your layer's attribute table: a client, another department, a contractor. Instead of copying it into Excel and formatting it by hand, export it in one step as a tidy workbook. Headers come from aliases, codes become labels, numbers keep their units and identifiers keep their leading zeros. You choose the look (Excel table or plain range, fonts, colours, borders, banded rows) with a live preview. Optional sheets: data dictionary, data origin (source, date, CRS), summary with totals and changes since the previous export. Columns with personal data (names, PESEL, e-mail, phone) are detected and left out or masked by default. You can add area, length, coordinates and a map link from the geometry. Also available as a Processing algorithm. Interface and file in 10 languages. Works right after installation, also from a ZIP file: the openpyxl library is bundled and used only when QGIS has none.
